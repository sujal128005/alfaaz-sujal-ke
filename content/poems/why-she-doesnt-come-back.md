---
title: Why She doesn't come back?
language: english
form: Poem
themes: [love, separation, memory]
emotions: [heartbreak, longing]
source: Shared by the author
---
In shadows of my heart, a question lies,
Why did she leave, beneath these tear-stained skies?
Her laughter lingers, a ghost in empty space,
Her touch a phantom on my yearning face.

Where once we walked with footsteps light and free,
A chasm yawns, a wound for all to see.
The words we shared, like petals in the breeze,
Swept away by whispers on the lonely trees.

Did my love falter, a flame too weak to burn?
Or did her spirit seek another path to turn?
The nights stretch long, a tapestry of pain,
Each star a tear that falls like endless rain.

Was it a word unspoken, a glance misunderstood?
The subtle shift of tides that changed our mood?
I search the echoes, the fragments left behind,
Some fleeting truth within my soul to find.

Oh, if only time could bend and rewind the past,
I'd hold her close, make every moment last.
But time flows onward, relentless is its stream,
And she remains a bittersweet, fading dream.

Perhaps one day the sun will chase this gloom,
And in my heart, a fragile hope may bloom.
Yet, still the question haunts with gentle sigh,
Why did she leave, and bid our love goodbye?
