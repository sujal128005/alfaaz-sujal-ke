Twilight paints the sky with gentle light,
Beneath the ancient oak, stars twinkle bright.
My heart races fast, palms begin to sweat,
This moment of courage, I'll not soon forget.
A thousand questions swirl in my head,
Words of love I've practiced, carefully said.
With a trembling voice, I look into your eyes,
A flicker of hope beneath the starry skies.
"Could this feeling grow?" my voice softly pleads,
Longing for connection, fulfilling both our needs.
A chance at a future, painted in vibrant hue,
My world forever changed, if you feel it too.
Time seems to stop, holding its breath for your reply,
My heart pounds a rhythm, a hope soaring high.
This moment hangs heavy, a future yet untold,
The answer I seek, worth more than pure gold.
