---
title: It’s Ending
language: english
form: Free verse
themes: [love, separation]
emotions: [heartbreak, acceptance]
source: Shared by the author
---
what do you call it?
fading echoes and
silent jingles

doesn't matter how long
how deep the feeling
unravelling slowly

bitter or hollow
away, turned sideways

unnecessary the flourish
no more pizzazz

waning
receding
diminishing
vanishing

retreat

with empty hands
