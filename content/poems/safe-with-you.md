The world spins wild, a maze where doubts take hold,
Fears whisper shadows, chilling stories told.
But in thy hand, a stillness doth descend,
A peaceful calm where all my worries end.
Thy strength becomes a beacon shining bright,
Dissolving fears like dreams within the night.
Anxieties that plagued my troubled heart,
Within thy tender embrace, they fall apart.
Thou art my sanctuary, my refuge sweet,
Where troubled thoughts face sure and swift defeat.
Thy love surrounds me, warmth against the chill,
Chasing shadows, all my doubt it instills.
With thee beside me, fears take rapid flight,
My heart finds solace in thy gentle light.
A sense of safety, love so true and deep,
The greatest comfort thy embrace doth keep.
