A faded tapestry
Of dreams once vibrant,
Now just a gleam.
Lost in the depths
Of time's abyss,
A forgotten memory,
A sweet kiss.
Echoes of laughter,
Whispers of love,
Now replaced by silence,
A mournful dove.
The world moves on,
Indifferent to the past,
While I'm left to cherish
Memories that can't last.
