A final glance, a tear no heart can hide,
This chapter ends, where gentle love once sighed.
Words catch like feathers, heavy in the air,
Farewell unspoken, a sorrow none can compare.
Where laughter echoed, silence steals its place,
Tears stain a path down my grief-stricken face.
Promises whispered fade into the past,
Moments we treasured, too precious to last.
Hands that once held tightly, now slowly slip away,
The warmth of connection begins to fade and fray.
Footsteps fade softly, a figure disappears,
Leaving behind memories to echo through the years.
A closing door thunders, a future undefined,
But remnants of our love will linger in my mind.
Though paths now diverge, and new journeys take flight,
The echoes of your presence remain a guiding light.
