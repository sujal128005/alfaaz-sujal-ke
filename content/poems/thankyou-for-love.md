---
title: Thankyou for love
language: english
form: Poem
themes: [love, separation]
emotions: [heartbreak, regret]
date: 2023-08-25
source: Shared on Facebook, 25 August 2023
---
With you, I was like a star in the sky,
Now I ask myself, why in love with you, oh why!
From morning's chirping till the evening's end,
We shared texts and calls, just message to send!
I believe our hearts beat as one, combined,
But in truth, in reality, only pain I find!
Your love, once thought true, now feels a lie,
I mistook your joy, and now I cry!
In my hands, your touch no more resides,
It seems the lines of fate, now far divide!
Since I've felt life without your sight,
Living now lacks its former light!
Thank you for the love, though it makes me sad,
In the end, not you, not he, not she, I was bad!!
