Sunlight paints the hall, where laughter rings so bright,
Two figures stand 'midst texts, in learning's golden light.
A stolen glance, a whispered jest we share,
Such tiny perfect moments, beyond all compare.
Fingers brush o'er page, a sweet spark takes its flight,
A playful tease ignites, a love burns ever bright.
In hushed library whispers, dreams we softly sow,
Building worlds of laughter, where shared hearts may grow.
A borrowed note, a tender touch, a smile so true,
Connection deepens with each smile shared by me and you.
In stolen moments, fleeting passage of the day,
A tapestry of sweetness, where friendship finds its way.
Late texts with sleepy smiles, our understanding grows,
A solace found in kinship, as connection gently flows.
Little moments, softly spun, thread a bond we shall adore,
The foundation of a feeling, blooming evermore.
