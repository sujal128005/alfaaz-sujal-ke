With trembling steps, I leave the past behind,
Seeking a balm for a heart shattered, unaligned.
The echoes of heartbreak begin to softly fade,
As a glimmer of hope offers what my spirit needs to be made.
Lessons etched upon my soul, a bittersweet gain,
Memories linger, reflecting tear-filled rain.
Though tears cleanse the wounds, a gentle, cleansing release,
Can time truly heal, restoring a lasting inner peace?
A chapter may close, but the story must proceed,
A journey of healing, where strength I will need.
New horizons unfurl, with hues of vibrant light,
Whispers of a future where joy takes flight.
With every breath, resilience begins to take its hold,
Dispelling the shadows, lifting the gloom, growing bold.
Moving forward bravely, though the path lies unseen,
Hey, can I find a way to leave this heartbreak, a spirit serene?
