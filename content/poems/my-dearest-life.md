We’ve been through it all, haven’t we? High fives on mountaintops, late-night talks with tear-stained cheeks. You throw me curveballs, some sweet, some sour, but I gotta admit, it keeps things interesting.

You know, life, what’s special with you? You’re a constant surprise. Every day is a new adventure, a chance to learn and grow. Even the ordinary moments hold magic if you know where to look.

Here's some verse for you:
“You paint the world in vibrant hues,
Then whisper secrets in the morning dew.
You break my heart, then mend it whole,
A teacher disguised as playing a role...

...You're fleeting moments, a whispered sigh,
A constant dance beneath the endless sky.
Though fears may cloud and shadows creep,
In your embrace, my soul finds sleep”

“Life's tapestry, woven with joy and strife, unfolds, a breathtaking gift.”

Things, I’m grateful in life:
Parents
Being a Poet
A Connection that uplifts: Friends
Himachal: Birth State

We’ll keep on dancing, hand in hand,
Life, you and I, across the land...

Thank you Life!
Yours alfaazsujalke
