In crowded chamber, 'midst a fleeting throng,
Our gazes met, a bond both swift and strong.
Stars shimmered in thine eyes, a tale untold,
A spark ignited, secrets to unfold.
Thy smile, a secret whispered in its gleam,
My heart takes flight, adrift upon a dream.
The world dissolves, and only we remain,
Two souls entwined where destinies entrain.
A warmth unfolds 'round me, a joyful start,
Thy gaze holds promise, treasures in thy heart.
Could this by chance a love ordained to be?
This meeting whispers sweet possibility.
Though crowds may swirl, we stand a world apart,
Two strangers bound by dictates of the heart.
In this locked gaze, where time and space are stilled,
A tale of love begins, yet to be fulfilled.
