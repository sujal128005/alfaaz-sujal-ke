Where waves crash with a rhythmic, endless roar,
Our footprints trace the sandy, timeless shore.
The sun paints warmth upon our tender skin,
A world of worries melts away within.
Here, time slows to a gentle, dreamlike pace,
Laughter echoes in this sacred, joyous space.
Secrets whispered to the soft ocean breeze,
Our souls find comfort, and our hearts find ease.
The salty air fills us with boundless delight,
A blessed escape from troubles, out of sight.
Hand in hand, we watch the seabirds take their flight,
A love that strengthens with each passing light.
The setting sun streaks gold across the tide,
Stories for tomorrow in its glow abide.
This is our haven, where our souls are free,
A paradise built for only you and me.
