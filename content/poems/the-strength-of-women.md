She walks a trail both worn and new,
Her strength a song, forever true.
Her hands have known both toil and grace,
Shaping worlds, a selfless pace.

Her eyes, twin pools of wisdom bright,
Have weathered storms, found guiding light.
They hold the tales of whispered years,
The silent triumphs, conquered fears.

Her voice, a haven in the night,
Carries hope with gentle might.
With words, she paints, inspires, and leads,
Sowing seeds for noble deeds.

Her heart, a wellspring deep and wide,
Flows with love, an endless tide.
Compassion blooms with every beat,
A boundless gift, so true and sweet.

Her spirit, like a rising flame,
Illuminates the path she came.
Unbound by time, her essence flies,
A beacon shining in the skies.

Though seasons shift, and landscapes change,
Her core remains, a steadfast range.
Woman of wonder, strength so rare,
Her legacy beyond compare.

We stand in awe, our voices rise,
Celebrating strength beneath the skies.
For in her steps, a future gleams,
Where boundless hearts fulfill their dreams.

This Women's Day, we raise our hands,
In honor of the strength she commands.
May her light forever glow,
A testament to all we know.
