[Verse 1]
Whenever I come close to you,
I live in you more than in myself.
Every breath ends up speaking of you,
Whether you say it or not, it all gets said.

[Pre-Chorus]
Even in your silences, there are words of mine,
Even in your tears, there is my smile.

[Chorus]
Close to you, just close to you,
I am far away from the world.
With you, only with you,
Even in every pain, I am light.

[Verse 2]
I had asked the moon for a light like you,
Once I found you, even pain turned to peace.
My paths are written in your hands,
And in my dreams now, it is only you.

[Pre-Chorus]
I live in the lines of your palms,
Beneath your eyelids, I am like a dream.

[Chorus]
Close to you, just close to you,
If I live, let me live just like this.
With you, only with you,
I would lose even myself.

[Bridge]
Without you, everything feels incomplete,
Without you, everything feels joyless.
Because you are here, everything is lovely,
Because you are here, everything is, my friend.

[Final Chorus]
Close to you, just close to you,
Wherever you are, that is where I am.
With you, only with you,
My life is written right here.
